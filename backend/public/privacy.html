USB Killswitch Zero-Trust Platform

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

USB Killswitch ("we", "our") provides zero-trust encrypted storage management. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use your data when authenticating through Google OAuth.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google OAuth, we only collect the following standard identity fields:

  • Email address: Used to associate your USB drive with your account and verify access permissions.
  • Basic profile name: Used solely to personalize the dashboard and mobile approval interface.

3. How We Use Information

  • To verify your identity as an authorized USB drive owner or requester.
  • To send out-of-band device approval prompts and enforce access-list rules.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties or advertisers.
  • We do not access your Google Drive, contacts, or any other private Google data.

4. Data Storage & Security

We use industry-standard encryption protocols (AES-256-GCM, TLS 1.3) to protect all sessions, tokens, and access logs.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository or admin support.
